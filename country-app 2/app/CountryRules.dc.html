VISITING

As a Standard Visitor you can stay up to 6 months at a time. You can't make the UK your main home through frequent or back-to-back visits.

TAX RESIDENCY

The UK uses the Statutory Residence Test. Its tax year runs 6 April to 5 April, and a day counts if you're here at midnight.

183+days: you're automatically UK resident
<16days: automatically not resident (fewer than 46 if you weren't resident in the last 3 years)
Betweenit depends on your ties: family, a home, work or past years here
